Flow: space on earth

Between artificial intelligence, accessibility and aesthetics: Flow, a kitchen for everyone!

Flow is a kitchen concept that integrates artificial intelligence, accessibility, and adaptive design to respond to contemporary living. The AI system learns users’ habits, anticipates needs, and optimizes daily activities, making the experience intuitive and personalized.

Accessibility is central to the project: traditional spatial limitations are rethought through a centralized layout that reduces movement and improves usability for people with different motor abilities. Transformable elements—mobile, translational, and rotational—allow the space to adapt to diverse needs.

Inspired by the fluid language of Zaha Hadid, Flow combines soft geometries and innovative morphologies, creating a smart, inclusive environment where functionality and emotion coexist.

[ 2023, Bachelor’s Thesis in Industrial Design, Politecnico di Bari ]
