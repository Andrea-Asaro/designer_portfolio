DADDARIO rosanna

MY work MOVES ACROSS EXHIBITION DESIGN, MATERIAL RESEARCH, AND THE relationship BETWEEN SPACE, BODY, AND PRACTICES

Hi, I’m Rosanna, a designer from Bari, currently based in Milan. I hold a Bachelor’s degree with honors in Industrial Design and a Master’s degree in Exhibition Design from Politecnico di Milano (May 2026).

Moving cities and starting over shaped the way I see design: I consider spaces as both functional systems and lived experiences, influencing how we feel, move, and connect.

I design narrative environments where every element plays a role in shaping a larger story, with a focus on everyday needs and meaningful interactions.

This portfolio reflects my journey and my way of thinking through design.

Skills and practice

A focused set of tools and practices that support the development, communication, and atmosphere of each project.

[Modeling & Prototyping]

3D modeling in Rhinoceros for complex geometries and prototype development workflows.

[CAD Design]

Technical drawings and construction details supporting design development and production processes.

[Rendering]

Visualisation in Enscape with post-production focused on materials, light, and atmosphere.

[Visual Communication]

Graphic design and visual systems using Adobe Suite for project presentations and identity development.
